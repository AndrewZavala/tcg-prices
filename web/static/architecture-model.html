Record lifecycle

One product line moves through analytical → operational → outcome states. Batch data is read-only; operational tables capture decisions and events.

Stage 1
Price snapshots
Point-in-time procurement & market prices loaded daily
read-only · versioned
→
Stage 2
Scored opportunity
Margin & ROI computed; ranked for action
read-only · refreshed
→
Stage 3
Inventory position
Commit to acquire; freeze expected economics at purchase
read-write · operational
→
Stage 4
Fulfillment event
Outbound shipment; decrement on-hand quantity
read-write · transactional
→
Stage 5
Attributed outcome
Realized margin when fulfillment closes (paid / delivered)
derived · reporting view
Analytical (pipeline-owned) Decision commit Operational events Outcome / BI layer
Expected (at commit) Margin frozen when inventory position is created — baseline for planning and forecast.
Realized (at close) Actual payout minus cost, rolled up from fulfillment events — true performance.
